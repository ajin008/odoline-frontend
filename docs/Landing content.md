## 5. Features

- **Stock and intake** — Record every car you buy, with seller details, refurbishment costs, and your margin. Always know what's in the yard and what each car really cost.
- **No missing papers** — Track RC, NOC, insurance, pollution certificate, and seller KYC for every car. A car can't be delivered until its required documents are in.
- **Leads and follow-ups** — Every enquiry is assigned to a salesperson with a follow-up date. Nothing slips, and you can see who is converting.
- **Bookings and delivery** — Prebook a car the moment a deal is won, so two salespeople can never sell the same car. Generate branded booking documents with amounts in lakhs and in words.
- **Staff attendance** — Staff clock in from their phone, only when they're at the showroom. See who's present, late, or absent at a glance.
- **Owner dashboards** — Enquiries, bookings, deliveries, and staff performance by day, week, or month. Your whole business in one screen.

Capability strip: Works on phone and computer · Install it like an app · Light and dark mode · Daily backups · Your data in its own private database
